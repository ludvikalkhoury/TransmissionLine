What is happening?

A voltage step launches from A and leaves its changed voltage behind it. At the load it reflects by ΓL; at the source it reflects by ΓS. The graph and schematic are both calculated by superposition of every delayed traveling step.

The RF antenna preset is an idealized 50 Ω resistive antenna input, not a full electromagnetic radiation simulation.

Physics: V⁺ = Vs·Z0/(Rs+Z0), ΓL = (RL−Z0)/(RL+Z0), ΓS = (Rs−Z0)/(Rs+Z0). Values use an ideal lossless transmission line.
